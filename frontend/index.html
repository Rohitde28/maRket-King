How It Works (ORR)

The engine analyses ES, MES, NQ every 30s (1m) / 60s (5m).

When the 4-factor ORR checklist is satisfied, a signal fires automatically.
✅ 4/4 factors → 85% probability (ENTER)
🟡 3/4 factors → 65% probability (CAUTION)
❌ <3 factors → No trade

NY window times for India (IST):
  Summer (EDT): 19:13–19:17 and 19:28–19:32

After a signal, the system checks every 30s whether SL or TP has been hit.
