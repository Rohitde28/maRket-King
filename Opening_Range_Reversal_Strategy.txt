Opening Range Reversal Strategy
Futures Day Trading System - Technical Specification
Document prepared for Developer Reference

1. Strategy Overview
This document describes an Opening Range Reversal (ORR) strategy applied to US equity index futures. The strategy identifies high-probability reversal points during the first 30-45 minutes after market open using a combination of timing windows, support/resistance zones, trend structure, and momentum signals.
The core principle: when the market makes a sharp, aggressive flush in the opening window and reaches a key level at a defined timing interval, it is highly likely to reverse. The trader enters in the direction of the reversal after trend-break confirmation, targeting 2-3x the initial risk.

The strategy does NOT enter randomly. It waits for specific time windows after the market opens, where reversals are statistically more common.

The 8:00 AM window (30 min after open) is considered the most reliable. The trader specifically watches for reversals at these exact times rather than entering at arbitrary moments.

4. Pre-Market Preparation
Before the market opens, the trader performs the following steps on a 15-minute or daily chart:
• Mark key overnight support and resistance levels where price may reverse
• Identify the previous day's high, low, and close - especially major swing points
• Note significant price gaps between yesterday's close and today's open
• Establish a directional bias based on trend structure (uptrend, downtrend, or range)
• Identify fair value gaps (FVGs) - areas of market imbalance from large, fast moves
• Mark the key timing windows (7:45 and 8:00) on the chart

Fair Value Gaps are highlighted using a custom NinjaTrader indicator. Multiple FVG signals stacking = stronger reversal conviction.

5. Entry Criteria (The 4-Factor Checklist)
All four factors must be present before entering a trade. Missing any one factor is reason to skip the trade.
Factor 1 - Strong Flush Into a Key Zone
The market must make an aggressive, directional move into a pre-marked support or resistance level. The move must be:
• Fast and large - not slow or choppy
• Reaching a pre-identified key support (for longs) or resistance (for shorts)
• Ideally accompanied by Fair Value Gap indicators firing
If the market is drifting or grinding without clear momentum, skip. Good reversal trades require volatile, fast-moving markets.
Factor 2 - Reversal Timing Alignment
The flush into the key zone must coincide with one of the defined timing windows (7:45 or 8:00 local time). The trader is highly alert during these periods and treats them as catalysts.
Factor 3 - Trend Structure Break
Since this is a reversal strategy, the current trend must show signs of failing. Evidence of trend break includes:
• Price breaking through a drawn trend line
• The sequence of Lower Highs / Lower Lows (downtrend) ending - market begins printing a Higher Low
• A 'head and shoulders' type pattern forming: a push to a new extreme, a pullback, and a failed second attempt at the extreme
The trader does NOT enter on the first sign of reversal. They wait for structural confirmation that the previous trend has broken before committing.
Factor 4 - Momentum Confirmation Candle
A large, decisive candle must break above the prior swing high (for longs) or below the prior swing low (for shorts). The entry is placed as a stop-market order triggered on the break of that level - NOT on a limit order trying to catch a pullback.
Entering on the break of the confirmation candle ensures momentum is already moving in the trade direction, even if the entry price is slightly worse than the absolute extreme.

6. Entry Execution
Entry is triggered using a stop-market order placed just above the prior swing high (long) or below the prior swing low (short). The trader uses a custom NinjaTrader tool that automatically calculates contract size based on:
• Pre-set dollar risk per trade (e.g. $500, $700, $1,400)
• Entry price and stop loss distance
• Number of contracts is calculated to match the target dollar risk

7. Stop Loss Placement
Stop loss is placed below the prior swing low (for longs) or above the prior swing high (for shorts) - specifically below the extreme of the flush that formed the reversal pattern.
Rationale: if price returns to that extreme and breaks it, the reversal thesis is invalidated and the original downtrend/uptrend is continuing. Exiting at that point preserves capital.

8.2 Management Rules
• Move stop to break-even once 2x-2.5x risk profit is reached
• Scale out partially (e.g. half position) at first target
• Trail the stop on remaining position below recent swing lows (for longs)
• If the trade's uptrend/downtrend line breaks, tighten stop aggressively
• Do NOT go to break-even too early - let the trade breathe
8.3 Trade Management Philosophy
The trader explicitly avoids over-managing trades. The most common mistake is exiting too early out of fear. The goal is to follow a pre-defined management plan and only act on specific chart signals, not emotional impulses.
Mental stop rule: if price makes a significant pullback and the uptrend structure breaks, the trader exits even without a stop-limit order being triggered.

10. Fair Value Gap (FVG) Usage
Fair Value Gaps are areas of market imbalance created by large, fast moves. They appear when a candle's body skips a price range entirely, leaving an 'unfilled' area on the chart. Key rules:
• FVGs are used as reversal targets, NOT as continuation signals
• Multiple stacked FVG indicators = stronger reversal signal (market is more overextended)
• Must pair FVG signals with a clear key support/resistance level - never trade FVGs in the middle of a range
• A single FVG with no key level support is a low-quality setup - skip it
The trader sizes up (larger risk) when FVGs align with a timing window AND a key level AND show H&S structure. All three together = highest-conviction trade.

11. When NOT to Trade
The strategy has strict discipline around avoiding low-quality setups:
• No flush into a key zone - skip
• Flush happens outside of timing windows - skip or wait for next window
• Market is choppy, slow-grinding, with no clear momentum - skip
• No trend line break or structural shift visible - skip
• Market has already made 2 large moves earlier in the session - be cautious, consider stopping
• Price is in the middle of a range with no clean key levels nearby - skip
• Trade not meeting the 4-factor checklist - skip, no exceptions

Not taking a trade every day is acceptable and expected. The strategy targets high-probability setups, not trade frequency. Waiting preserves capital and mental clarity.

12. Psychology & Discipline Notes
The following behavioral rules are an explicit part of the strategy:
• Visualize the setup before market open - review past winning trades for pattern reinforcement
• Do not react emotionally - only act when the 4-factor checklist is confirmed
• Avoid FOMO entries - if the entry moment passes, wait for the next setup
• Do not think about dollar amounts while in a trade - focus on chart signals
• If the market is not acting as expected, stop for the day - do not force trades
• Accept losses as part of the process when entries followed the rules

14. Decision Flowchart Summary
The following summarizes the go/no-go decision process:
1. Pre-market: Mark key support/resistance zones, gaps, FVG areas, and timing lines on chart.
2. Market open (7:30): Observe price action. Do NOT enter. Watch direction and momentum.
3. At 7:45 or 8:00: Is price at a key level? Is there a strong flush? Are FVGs firing?
4. If YES: Is the current trend breaking? Is a H&S or double bottom/top forming?
5. If YES: Wait for confirmation candle breaking prior swing high/low.
6. Enter with stop-market order. Set stop loss. Calculate contract size via risk tool.
7. Manage trade: move to break-even at 2R, scale out at 2R-3R, trail stop on remainder.
8. Exit fully when: limit target hit, trend breaks, or trailing stop triggered.
9. Evaluate: Did entry follow the rules? Log the trade regardless of outcome.
